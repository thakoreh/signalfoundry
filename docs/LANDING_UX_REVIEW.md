## Preserve and refine

Keep the consistent logo, restrained borders, clear display headings, and single dominant action. Email status remains visible without implying guaranteed delivery or customer intent. The message preview and CSV are better proof than invented testimonials, counters, or autonomous-agent claims.

Two optional refinements remain. First, the phone workflow example spends most of a screen on the seller-profile context before showing the shortlist. Shorten that context or provide a direct route to the list example, while keeping website entry prominent. Second, remove the repeated “Start with your website” from the hero supporting sentence; the heading already says it. These are polish opportunities, not blockers or evidence of increased conversion.

## What happens after search

Keep one coherent workspace: **customer list → message drawer → edit/copy or selected CSV → return to the list or adjust a new search**. There is no need for campaign, agent, or CRM pages to complete this task. A later kept/contacted state may support repeat work if pilot users need it; that would be a new feature and would not prove that outreach happened. Sending, CRM sync, autonomous research, and guaranteed verified contacts are outside the current product.

## Accessibility and verification limits

The reviewer inspected fresh screenshots and the responsive/semantic implementation, and independently ran 13 landing/intake/motion tests plus 14 result/draft tests; all passed. Native table roles/captions remain when phone rows reflow. The draft footer has safe-area padding, the phone editor uses 16px text, and reduced-motion overrides disable transitions. Landing content is server-rendered and the example disclosure works natively; without JavaScript, the signup form remains available, with a note that the website must be entered again. The finite preview reveal does not hide the default static content.

Parent Chrome checks reported no horizontal page overflow after the fix at 390px and 320px, Copy inside the narrow viewport, and Escape restoring trigger focus. These are browser checks, not physical-device evidence. Safari/iOS, a physical phone keyboard, screen readers, 200–400% zoom, measured contrast, and every loading/error state remain unverified. Static captures cannot certify motion timing or WCAG compliance. No analytics, user interviews, production signup, payment, retention, or conversion outcome was observed.
