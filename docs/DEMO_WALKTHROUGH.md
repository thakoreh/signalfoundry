# Five-minute product walkthrough

Use fictional demo mode when presenting the prototype. Say upfront that the companies, people and signals in that mode are fixtures.

1. **Define the customer.** Load the demo workspace, then open the ideal customer profile. Show the target industries, buyer roles and exclusions. Edit one target and save it to demonstrate real persistence.
2. **Run a campaign.** Create a fictional demo campaign and run its research. Explain that real campaigns currently start from supplied public company domains, not a paid contact database.
3. **Inspect the decision.** Open a top-ranked account. Show the fit score breakdown, evidence excerpts, source URLs and retrieval dates. Differentiate the facts from judgments, and highlight missing information.
4. **Review the contact boundary.** Show the contact status. There is no guessed email or simulated “verified” badge.
5. **Take the next step.** Shortlist the account, create an outreach draft and inspect its evidence basis. Copy it for human review or export CSV. No message is sent.

## What this proves

The application connects profile editing, persisted campaigns, evidence-backed account review and a human-reviewed next step in one working UI. The architecture keeps cheap bounded decisions separate from discovery, data verification and writing.

## What it does not prove

It does not yet establish real-world sourcing coverage, contact accuracy, deliverability, conversion rate, customer willingness to pay or calibrated decision quality. Those need independent tests and customer feedback, not more polished demo fixtures.
