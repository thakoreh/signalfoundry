## What they actually pitch
- Find accounts showing buying signals, identify the relevant contact, and expose a 'Why this account' explanation.
- Ground the first message in account research and saved seller/product details; edit messages before launch.
- Email and LinkedIn sequences, follow-ups, account-level schedules/caps and a combined reply inbox.
- Campaign launch requires approval. Their Copilot mode additionally claims approval of each message; Autopilot sends scheduled messages. Do not conflate campaign approval with per-message approval.
- Their email page says new inboxes begin with a lower daily cap, ramping while bounces stay low. No independent operational test was performed.

## Useful principles for SignalFoundry within existing scope
1. Make the source, observation date, role and relevance behind each account obvious—not an unexplained fit or intent number.
2. Keep buyer evidence and saved seller offer facts adjacent to message preparation; write a factual opener and a small relevant question. No invented pain, fake familiarity, flattery or unsupported claims.
3. Connect selected lead → reviewed exact message → real reply → explicit next action. The return queue and reply consequences matter more than cosmetic cold-email templates.
4. Show honest unavailable/manual/connected boundaries. Preserve manual approval, suppression, recipient-basis and region safeguards; do not add LinkedIn automation, monitoring services or live sends for competitive parity.
5. Evaluate results with actual contacted/replied/meeting/held/won events, not copied-message counts or invented response rates.
