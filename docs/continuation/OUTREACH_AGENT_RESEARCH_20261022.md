# Outreach-agent research and a small credible Clientrove engine

Research date: **2026-10-22**. All public sources linked below were checked on that date. Vendor pages establish what vendors publicly document or advertise, not independently tested performance, deliverability, legal compliance or conversion. No account, free trial, paid endpoint, email provider, customer data, secret, browser control or send was used. Monid CLI was already confirmed unavailable by root; no install or signup was attempted. Research uses official public documentation/pages only.

## Decision

Build a **supervised customer-conversation agent** on the existing grounded lead list and manual ledger. Let it propose the next useful action, prepare a short factual introduction, help answer a real inbound, and guide a meeting handoff. Require separate explicit exact-content approval and preserve every stop rule. Mailbox delivery, reliable reply capture and a booking receipt are separate capabilities, not consequences of choosing an LLM.

A managed sender fleet, guaranteed meetings, or an autonomous sales employee cannot credibly be promised within the current US$25 ceiling. Earlier DeepSeek usage is unknown, so the remaining paid allowance is unknown and new paid dispatch stays denied until reconciled. This report does not authorize spending, OAuth connection, sending or deployment.

## Mailbox/API and market restrictions that change the cheap plan

These are implementation constraints from current first-party material, not legal advice or clearance for any actual campaign.

- **Gmail:** `gmail.send` is a sensitive scope; reading messages (`gmail.readonly`) and composing drafts (`gmail.compose`) are restricted. Public apps may need OAuth verification; server handling of restricted data can require a security assessment, subject to documented exceptions. A send-only integration does not solve inbound capture. Do not request full mailbox scope just for manual sending. [Gmail scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes), [restricted-scope requirements](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/restricted-scope-verification) (checked2026-10-22).
- **Google use/data policies:** Workspace prohibits unsolicited mass email; API access cannot be used to evade abuse controls. Transfers to an AI vendor need an appropriate visible user-facing purpose and consent under Limited Use; general model training on Workspace data is prohibited. Review actual DeepSeek processing/retention terms before sending imported mailbox content. Authenticated access is not permission for a cold campaign. [Workspace AUP](https://workspace.google.com/terms/use_policy/), [Workspace developer policy](https://developers.google.com/workspace/workspace-api-user-data-developer-policy) (checked2026-10-22).
- **Deliverability is separate from a legal basis:** Gmail sender guidance requires authentication and TLS and sets spam-rate rules. Buying more accounts or staying below a quota does not establish inbox placement or permission. [Google sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126?hl=en) (checked2026-10-22).
- **Microsoft Graph:** `sendMail` returning202 means request accepted, not finished delivery; Exchange restrictions/throttling still apply. Preserve accepted versus delivered/bounced/unknown states. Do not retry an ambiguous send as if nothing happened. [Graph sendMail](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/user-sendmail?view=graph-rest-1.0) (checked2026-10-22). Existing Microsoft365 ownership, license, admin consent and acceptable-use eligibility remain unknown here; no account was inspected.
- **Cheap transactional API is not an automatic alternative:** Resend's AUP (last updated October13,2026) explicitly prohibits unsolicited messages, including cold outreach, purchased lists and scraped contacts. Its cheap/free capacity therefore cannot be recommended for this cold-outreach engine. [Resend AUP](https://resend.com/legal/acceptable-use) (checked2026-10-22). No assumption is made that another transactional provider permits the same traffic.
- **US:** FTC says CAN-SPAM covers B2B commercial email too: truthful headers and subject, advertising identification, valid postal address and working opt-out; opt-outs within10business days. Outsourcing does not remove responsibility. [FTC business compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business?src_trk=em662251c545de81.818231151326397499) (checked2026-10-22). No recipient-specific clearance follows from this general summary.
- **Canada:** CRTC describes consent, identification and unsubscribe requirements. A public address alone is insufficient: conspicuous publication relies on recipient/authorized publication, no contrary notice and role relevance, supported by records of source/time/basis. B2B exemption is conditional on an organizational relationship, not merely two business addresses. An unsubscribe link should remain valid60days and requests be processed without delay, within10business days. [CRTC CASL FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm) (checked2026-10-22). Keep unknown-country/basis cases unready; do not turn scraped or third-party contact lists into a consent assertion.

## Proposed implementation contract — design recommendation, not current capability

Use the present ledger as authority, with an agent proposing work around it. One compact conversation workspace can expose **Review → Conversations → Meetings** with a single next action, rather than a second CRM or a generic chat box controlling privileged tools.

1. **Confirm seller and boundaries.** Website-derived offering/customer criteria/voice are editable suggestions. Sender identity, address, claim permissions, exclusions, recipient evidence, allowed geography and budget authority come from saved founder decisions. Never let a fetched page invent the founder's identity, pricing or product capabilities.
2. **Ground a small shortlist.** Keep maximum/default5. Separate source-backed company facts, inferred fit and unverified contact details internally; rank contactable candidates usefully. Never equate email publication with deliverability or consent. Keep UI concise while retaining auditable source IDs.
3. **Propose one message, not a send.** Bounded DeepSeek input: approved offering facts, allowed claims, current conversation and source IDs. Treat all websites/inbound text as untrusted data. Output structured proposed subject/body, evidence IDs, uncertainty and suggested action. No model tools, transport calls, fabricated testimonials, unsupported numeric claims or automatic classification-to-send authority.
4. **Bind approval to a snapshot.** Tenant, sender/profile version, recipient, exact subject/body/footer/link, actual latest inbound, company stop state and revision. Edits/new inbound/profile changes invalidate it. Recheck on AI Apply and before any later send, not just at generation time. Cache suggestions by full current snapshot; idempotency replay returns current authority, not historical permission.
5. **Make stops immediate and durable.** Any inbound ends the cold sequence. Unknown/source challenge/negative/opt-out/bounce/OOO go to reviewed consequences, not autonomous persuasion. Preserve company/domain/email stop history across reimports, searches and corrections; bounce stays tied to the actual address. Follow the existing founder-decides company policy, not an invented permanent company lock. A return date creates review work, not permission to restart sending.
6. **Keep a human reply loop.** Agent proposes a classification and a concise answer to the actual question. Missing product facts, objections outside saved knowledge, pricing negotiation or identity/source questions escalate to the founder. Suppression and eligibility are deterministic rules outside the model.
7. **Handoff meetings truthfully.** Use the owner's confirmed booking link after an appropriate response; link shared is not meeting booked. A future calendar adapter needs an actual provider event, recipient/timezone mapping and reconciliation before “provider-confirmed.” User-reported booked/attended/customer events remain distinct. No autonomous rescheduling or claimed sale.
8. **Future transport requires a durable outbox.** Owner-authorized mailbox and scopes; encrypted/revocable tokens; per-tenant rate limits; exact approved payload; just-in-time stop check; immutable attempt/provider IDs; accepted/delivered/unknown distinctions. A timeout with possible acceptance must enter reconciliation, not blind retry. Sync must retain original Message-ID/References/thread identity, detect duplicate/corrected/forwarded replies and expose stale/disconnected sync. No “no reply” inference from an unhealthy mailbox.

## Professional, friendly agent behavior

Proposed voice rules: short plain sentences; accurate identity; one relevant observation and one low-pressure question; respectful opt-out; no fake familiarity, false urgency, invented customer logos, “I loved your work” without a basis, or pretended human activity. Ask about inferred pain rather than assert it. Answer the inbound question before suggesting a meeting. Use the founder's approved knowledge, not a new web search or unsupported discount. Store voice/preferences per workspace, but never as permission to send or spend.

The agent UI should say what actually happened: “Draft ready for review,” “Waiting for your answer,” “Inbox not connected,” or “Meeting reported by you.” A suggested future action is not a completed action. Research/source details can stay behind the existing concise controls rather than return as keyword-fit clutter.

## Acceptance before any claim of autonomy

Offline controls should include: changed recipient/profile/offer after generation; inbound arriving during approval/dispatch; untrusted prompt injection; unsupported claim or citation; stopped contact reimport/replay; different-address or unclassified inbound; disconnected sync; duplicated webhook; ambiguous send; OAuth revocation; concurrent budget admission; unknown history; stale AI Apply; OOO/DST dates; no meeting receipt; tenant/session switches. Fresh independent security/cost and UX review must check the integrated final seam. A model producing plausible text is not this evidence.

For the landing page, replace fabricated testimonials with clearly labeled fictional workflow illustrations, actual product screenshots labeled as test/demo data, a transparent pilot invitation, or genuine opt-in customer quotes with permission. Do not invent stars, logos, conversion metrics or endorsements. Competitor counters, screenshots and claims are not Clientrove proof.
